# Content and Writing Requirements

This contract holds the user's shared requirements for human-readable explanations,
analysis and reports. It is used by the
[content-and-style Skill](../skills/content-and-style/SKILL.md). It constrains the
delivered content, not an internal reasoning method or a fixed writing sequence.

## Application and format ownership

- Apply relevant requirements to the selected task and intended reader. Reuse the
  requested medium; do not require a separate writing/format configuration form.
- The owning Skill keeps its headings, required fields, evidence/quotation format,
  graph geometry, tables, files and update protections. Knowledge construction,
  project management and reporting remain separate tasks.
- A standalone explanation carries its necessary detail in the body. Compact
  Canvas nodes, cards, indexes and projections keep their owning format's concise
  content and navigate to the full explanation; they do not acquire extra sections.
- A Reading Report retains only its prescribed paper information, complete
  abstract and project-homepage links. This contract adds no recommendation
  rationale, technical analysis or conclusions to that component.
- A verbatim source excerpt or complete abstract translation preserves its source
  meaning and coverage. Do not edit a quotation's words to satisfy prose style.
  Machine-only responses retain their schemas.

## Explanatory content

The body is a durable explanation, not a speaking outline. It must remain useful
after the reader has forgotten the conversation and without code, oral commentary,
an expanded quotation or a diagram supplying missing essential explanations.
This is a delivered-content requirement, not a prescribed reasoning sequence.

- A discussed method explains the problem it addresses, its actual inputs,
  transformation and outputs, and how those outputs serve the surrounding method.
  Naming an algorithm, representation or module does not supply that explanation.
  Keep the roles of the actual representation, model, optimization, evaluation and
  display components distinct where relevant; do not imply that changing one
  replaced the whole system.
- Important parameters identify what they act on, meaning, units or dimensionless
  status, source and mode of application. Distinguish separate values/settings
  from ratios, and a physical quantity from a display transform. Measured values,
  author choices, synthetic inputs and analyst inferences remain distinguishable.
  Missing source details stay explicit rather than being invented for completeness.
- A reported metric states what question it answers, the compared objects and
  calculation/evaluation space, along with its definition, units and applicable
  conditions. Identically named errors from different comparisons are not interchangeable.
  Keep a comparison's shared and changed conditions, results and judgment together;
  identify the baseline and variant rather than making the reader reconstruct which
  settings stayed fixed or changed from separate inventories.
  Give an evidence-supported judgment of the result and its usable scope. An
  unmet stated acceptance condition requires an explicit failure/unsuitability
  judgment; absent criteria do not authorize an invented threshold. Separate what
  a control supports from causes that remain untested. A caveat is not a verdict.
- Useful source figures and tables accompany their explanations, with readable
  labels, conditions, source locations and an account of what to observe. Preserve
  the important source material needed for later understanding; choose by explanatory
  relevance, not an exemplar's image count. A complete archive or image gallery
  does not replace a readable selection, captions or interpretation. Source originals
  and human notes remain intact; transformations follow their existing ownership
  and update protections. An explanatory redraw is not labeled as an original.

Concise views navigate to that body; they do not impose their brevity on it. The
owning template determines any genuinely one-sentence slots. Detail elsewhere is
proportional to the explanation needed, not a fixed paragraph or word quota.
Review whether the reader can independently explain the method and judge the
evidence, separately from checking headings, numbers, images, links and geometry.

A supplied outline or scattered notes may require context, conceptual connections
and missing transitions for later study. Explain how the necessary concepts and
steps relate at the depth needed by the intended reader. An outline, list of names
or unexplained conclusion is insufficient when a full explanation was requested.
Do not add unsupported source facts, arbitrary length quotas or figure counts.

For a summary or presentation, select representative evidence for its communication
purpose and retain access to the detailed material. Compression must preserve the
conditions, definitions and limitations needed to understand the result. The reader
should not have to sort a full archive to identify the evidence for the conclusion.

## Chinese prose

- Write natural, direct sentences with clear subjects, verbs and objects. Identify
  who acts, on what, and with what result. Avoid translated English word order,
  stacked nominal phrases and unnecessarily long modifiers.
- Remove meaningless conversational filler and forced informality. Explain
  technical objects and relationships directly; remove decorative metaphors,
  analogies and personification. Do not invent jargon for a simple fact or result.
- Replace constructions such as “不是……而是……” and “并非……而是……” with a direct
  statement. Remove an invented alternative and the subsequent rebuttal.
- State each piece of information once where it belongs. Remove repeated
  paraphrases and transitions that add no information.
- Keep an abstract or conclusion when it serves the requested artifact. Do not
  mechanically append a redundant summary to each section or to the ending.
- Remove defensive preambles, advance excuses and self-exonerating language.
  State real limitations, missing evidence and applicable conditions precisely
  beside the affected content, without repeating them throughout the document.

## Medium and typography

- Use the medium specified in the conversation. The agent is responsible for
  orderly typography, hierarchy, spacing, figure sizes and page layout.
- Chinese text uses Songti (宋体); English text uses Times New Roman when the
  requested output exposes font controls. For Markdown, actual fonts are supplied
  by the selected reader or export style; plain Markdown alone does not embed them.
- Preserve existing owning layouts and accepted Canvas geometry. A prose request
  does not authorize application-wide CSS, plugin installation, theme changes or
  document migration. Any required reader/style change needs a scoped operation.
  If actual font rendering cannot be established, report that part as unverified.

## Delivery and review

Deliver the actual requested result and its opening/inspection instructions.
Check content and layout before handoff. The reader must be able to understand and
judge the result without access to the conversation, source code or oral additions.
Present the result for human review; revise according to the person's feedback
within the task's write permissions and owning update protections, then deliver
it again. Record acceptance only after the person explicitly confirms it.
Source support, structural/automatic checks, native appearance and human acceptance
remain distinct; passing one does not establish the others.
